# Writing conventions (learned from corrections)

Rules the writers and the reviewer both follow. Each one traces to a mistake caught in this repo (see `corrections.jsonl`).

1. Verify every calculation by hand before publishing. Two early errors were an EVM question with a nonsense option and a mitigation question with the wrong answer marked.
2. All four options must be plausible to someone who half knows the topic. No joke options.
3. One BEST answer. If two options could be defended, rewrite the stem.
4. Stems must contain every fact needed. Do not rely on outside assumptions.
5. Domain tags follow the July 2026 outline: risk, change control, compliance, governance, issues, continuous improvement, organisational change and external environment are Business Environment.
6. Roughly 60% of items should involve agile or hybrid situations; 40% predictive.
7. The explanation must say why the tempting wrong answer fails, not only why the right one works.
8. Never copy or paraphrase real exam questions or commercial prep-book questions. Write from the outline's task list.
9. Correct-answer positions vary; the simulator also shuffles at run time.
